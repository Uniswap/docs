---

`ERC4626WrapperHook` exposes an [ERC-4626](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626) vault as a Uniswap v4 pool. One side of the pool is the vault's share token (the **wrapper currency**), the other is `vault.asset()` (the **underlying currency**). A swap in one direction deposits the underlying into the vault and returns freshly minted shares; a swap in the other direction redeems shares and returns the underlying. Conversion always happens at the vault's reported exchange rate, so a swap settles exactly as `vault.deposit` or `vault.redeem` would, reached through the swap interface routers and aggregators already speak to.

The pool holds no liquidity and charges no fee. It is a conversion venue, not a market: no price discovery, no LP position, and no slippage beyond the vault's own rounding. All swap-time logic lives in `beforeSwap`, which absorbs the entire swap and returns a `BeforeSwapDelta` describing the conversion.

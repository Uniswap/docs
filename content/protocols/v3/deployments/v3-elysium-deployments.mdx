## Universal Router

The `UniversalRouter` contract is the current preferred entrypoint for ERC20 and NFT swaps, replacing, among other contracts, `SwapRouter02`. The Elysium `UniversalRouter` addresses are listed in the table above and on the [Uniswap v4 deployments](/docs/protocols/v4/deployments) page.

## Uniswap Pool Deployments

Every Uniswap pool is a unique instance of the `UniswapV3Pool` contract and is deployed at its own unique address. The contract source code of the pool will be auto-verified on the explorer. You can browse [Elysium contracts on the explorer](https://elysium.kinetiq.xyz/explorer) to confirm any deployment.

You can look up the address of an existing pool on [Uniswap Info](https://info.uniswap.org/#/) or by calling the `getPool` function on the `UniswapV3Factory` contract.

```solidity
getPool("0xa0b86991c6218b36c1d19d4a2e9eb0ce3606eb48", "0xc02aaa39b223fe8d0a0e5c4f27ead9083c756cc2", 3000)
```

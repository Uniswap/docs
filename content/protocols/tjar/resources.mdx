## Repository

[Uniswap/tjar](https://github.com/Uniswap/tjar) holds the contracts, tests, and deployment scripts. It is licensed AGPL-3.0-only.

TJAR derives from [Uniswap/protocol-fees](https://github.com/Uniswap/protocol-fees), pruned to the Token Jar and Releaser contracts and their supporting base and interface files. Everything specific to the Uniswap protocol fee and governance lives in that upstream repository.
